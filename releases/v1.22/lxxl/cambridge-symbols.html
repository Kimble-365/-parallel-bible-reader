Corrections and conventions

The superscript a in Reg. Gr. 17ᵃ belongs to the printed identification of f; it is not a corrector label attached to f. OCR must not silently discard it.

The plain b (19) must be distinguished from the adjacent italic b (108), Rome, Vat. Gr. 330, which the edition says supplies lacunae and some hexaplaric notes. A particular reading's dependence on a supplement still needs detailed coverage checking.

The p. v N.B. defines * as the original writing of the scribe. Thus q* at Genesis 1:3 has that meaning in this edition. This identifies a convention; it does not establish a physical hand through independent manuscript inspection.

Parenthetical numbers are explicitly Holmes–Parsons numbers. No modern Rahlfs or Göttingen equivalence is inferred. Generic correction labels a/b exclude B A D E F S, whose individual entries govern their notation.
