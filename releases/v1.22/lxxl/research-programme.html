Research programme and readiness

2 November 2026 · LXXL text scope and apparatus

LXXL is a Genesis research preview, not an established critical edition or a replacement for Göttingen. Technical file/index verification is distinct from textual-critical validation. The current independent ancient manuscript-image collation count is zero.

Three phases and acceptance criteria

  1. Document and digitize printed Swete volumes. Verify actual title/edition statements, contents, page sequences and item-specific terms; preserve source checksums and line/page coordinates. Prepare reviewed diplomatic gold samples spanning Greek, diacritics, Latin, sigla, small apparatus type and damage. Compare at least two plausible OCR/layout engines on identical held-out samples and report errors separately by stratum. Raw OCR stays a proposal.
  2. Göttingen-anchored Genesis 1:1–5 pilot. Inspect the relevant volume’s introduction, exact text/apparatus loci, witness coverage, critical signs and supplements. Build an edition-specific siglum/hand concordance, record primary manuscript evidence where actually inspected, and obtain qualified review. The official catalogue entry for Wevers’s Genesis (1974) and related books is a bibliographic starting point; it is not proof those volumes or apparatuses have been read. No modern apparatus is copied in bulk here.
  3. Expand book by book. Establish each book’s editions, Greek textual forms, witnesses, apparatus conventions, rights and expertise before expansion. Keep distinct Greek forms and versional roles separate. Do not harmonize them into one asserted original LXX.

Evidence and explicit gaps

The same eight units now have twenty structured reported-reading records. Each records the immediate source and locus, exact reported label where applicable, source hashes, certainty class and interpretation. Diplomatic forms, manuscript folios, identified hands and specialist approval remain null or false where not established. A digital baseline token anchor is not manuscript evidence. Unknown agreement coverage is null, not a claim that no other witnesses agree.
