Editorial status

The Greek reading text is the Brenton-labelled digital Genesis baseline, retained provisionally. This is not a completed critical edition or an independently reconstructed Old Greek text. No registered variant is silently adopted. There are 1,531 Greek source verse slots and 1,533 in each comparandum. Genesis 31 and 35 have unresolved Greek numbering; comparisons follow source labels, not proven passage equivalence. The Reuben episode in Greek 35:21 corresponds to Hebrew 35:22.

Swete and WLC are separately installed reader editions; their source histories and numbering remain distinct. No newly discovered GitHub corpus is counted as an independent ancient witness. The apparatus below has eight registered units, not all known variants. Manuscript images have not been independently collated.

Qumran and rabbinic evidence

Qumran units are not flattened into a continuous reading text here: lacunae, uncertainty and restorations require separate evidence. In 4Q10 at Genesis 2:2 the seventh-day ordinal is fully restored in the imported transcription, not preserved support for that reading. Megillah 9a is later reception testimony, not a direct original Greek manuscript witness.

The apparatus prints φως 1°] pr το p. The article is an addition before the first occurrence of φῶς, not before its second occurrence.
