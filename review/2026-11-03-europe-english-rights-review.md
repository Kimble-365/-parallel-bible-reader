## Special cases

- **dan1931** — Initially labelled "Project Gutenberg redistribution terms; NT
  Public Domain; OT © 1931" from the historical notice. Re-checked
  2026-11-04 and relabelled **Public Domain**: Det Danske Bibelselskab's
  official copyright page claims rights only to the 1992 and 2020
  translations; the society has confirmed the 1931 OT is public domain
  (confirmation reported by the Danish-Bible-OT-1931 project); Project
  Gutenberg hosts the OT (ebook #2144); BibleGateway treats the text as
  public domain in the United States. The historical © 1907, 1931 notice
  and 1990s Project Gutenberg "Small Print" remain in the source archive
  for provenance.
- **CC BY-ND / CC BY-NC-ND editions** (16 total) — format conversion only;
  no textual revision. Display omits headings, notes, cross references and
  front/back matter; the complete notices stay in the source archives.
- **engwebp / engoeb** — duplicate ids of already-present English editions;
  not re-imported.
