# 1.28 introduction and prayer resources

Checked 7 November 2026. The application serves these small English editorial resources locally; they are not Bible editions, pronunciation resources, scholarly apparatus or medical diagnoses.

## Original resources

Three short guides (Bible overview, Christian theology, salvation) and eight prayers were written for this project. Scripture references are navigation links, not copied text from a protected translation. No source Bible bytes are changed. The theological guide identifies a historic Christian/Nicene frame and acknowledges differences between churches. Faith prayers are expressions of trust rather than mechanically effective formulas. Healing/protection prayers seek care and help without promising cures or diagnosing demonic oppression.

## Historic selections

- Augustine, *Confessions* 1.1.1: the short restless-heart sentence, newly rendered in English for this project from the ancient Latin. Latin source verified at https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/conf/text1.html . Modern commentary and other translators’ English are not copied.
- Augustine, *Confessions* 10.29.40: the short “da quod iubes et iube quod vis” petition, newly rendered in English. Latin source verified at https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/conf/text10.html . No modern critical apparatus/commentary is reused.
- The Jesus Prayer: only the short common traditional formula (12 words), verified at https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/spirituality/prayer-fasting-and-almsgiving/the-jesus-prayer . This is an attributed short quotation, not a license to redistribute the publisher’s article. No single historic author is claimed.

Only these stated short selections are included. No broad reuse permission is inferred for the source websites. Attribution and source links remain visible and accompany resource copying. These editorial additions do not relicense existing Bible texts, assets or project code. They neither alter the 84-edition baseline nor incorporate the unavailable 1.29 package.
