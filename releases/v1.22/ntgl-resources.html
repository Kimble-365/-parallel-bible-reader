Tischendorf printed text and apparatus

Archive.org catalogue candidates: volume I (1869) and volume II (1872). On 1 November 2026, a visual check confirmed volume I as the 1869 editio octava critica maior and inspected the John opening on printed pages 739–742. John 1:4 prints ἐστιν; its apparatus discusses ἐστιν/ἦν on page 742. A full stop after γέγονεν in printed John 1:3 is absent from the imported digital verse. Page-specific findings and image checksums record this limited check. Sinaiticus manuscript images and volume II remain unchecked; the bounded Bezae check is described below; specialist review is pending. These scans are not the separate 1877 critica minor, and no unverified OCR is installed as an apparatus.

Bezae: John 1:1–5 source sample

IGNTP version 1.03 supplies 60 source word occurrences from folio 104v, linked provisionally to lexical notes. Two target sequences, ουδεν at 1:3 and εστιν at 1:4, were checked against the Cambridge image. One-word segmentation of ουδεν comes from the transcription; the image is in scriptio continua. No final reading is adopted. Source text, lexical notes, provenance and limits.

Show the original-reading word projection

This unaccented projection joins TEI words with spaces and omits punctuation. Source abbreviations and ενεγετο are retained; it is not a diplomatic transcription.

  1. εν αρχη ην ο λογος και ο λογος ην προς τον θν και θς ην ο λογος
  2. ουτος ην εν αρχη προς τον θν
  3. παντα δι αυτου ενεγετο και χωρις αυτου εγενετο ουδεν ο γεγονεν
  4. εν αυτω ζωη εστιν και η ζωη ην το φως των ανθρωπων
  5. και το φως εν τη σκοτια φαινει και η σκοτια αυτο ου κατελαβεν
