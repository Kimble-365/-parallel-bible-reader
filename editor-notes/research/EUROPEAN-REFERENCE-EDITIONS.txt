EUROPEAN REFERENCE EDITIONS — PARALLEL BIBLE READER 1.29
========================================================
Date: 2026-11-03
Status: reference material imported; available for consultation

SCOPE
-----
Collection of redistributable English and European Bible editions for
side-by-side consultation with The Way Version. The material lives in the
Parallel Bible Reader (release 1.29) and is packaged under
original-documents/reference-editions/parallel-reader-1.29/.

Target languages from the original request: Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian,
Danish, Icelandic, plus English and other European languages.

METHOD
------
1. Enumerated the eBible.org Scriptures catalogue (1,552 rows; 1,295 marked
   redistributable) on 2026-11-03.
2. Filtered to 133 English/European candidate editions; downloaded and read
   each edition's details page and USFM archive.
3. Skipped 13 candidates already present in the reader under another id
   (duplicate mapping is recorded in the review register).
4. Excluded 4 candidates whose notices did not grant general electronic
   redistribution (reasons in the review register and the rights review).
5. Imported 116 editions through a deterministic plain-verse pipeline:
   USFM -> verse rows -> per-book gzip packs with recorded SHA-256 for both
   the expanded and packed bytes.
6. Verified the result with an audit script and a headless-browser runtime
   check (all 116 new editions load; preserved editions still load; search
   and offline save work).

LIMITATIONS
-----------
- Coverage is whatever the source edition contains at the snapshot date:
  several editions are New Testament only; the Icelandic 316-biblían
  currently contains Mark only.
- Versification is the source's own. Cross-edition alignment is not
  asserted; the reader labels this per edition.
- No-derivatives editions (CC BY-ND, CC BY-NC-ND) received format conversion
  only. Words and punctuation are the source's; headings, notes, cross
  references and front/back matter are omitted from display but remain in
  the source archives.
- The import is technical ingestion, not scholarly verification of the
  underlying texts.

EXCLUSIONS
----------
- swef: copyright notice only; no explicit redistribution licence on the
  reviewed source page.
- engnet: separate licence review required; verse-only display would omit
  the integral translators' notes.
- ukr1996: the source permission imposes a doctrinal condition on
  redistributors, so it is not a general public redistribution grant.
- spav1602p: the reviewed notice permits printing but does not clearly grant
  electronic redistribution.
