Five complete 27-book digital comparison editions are installed. Sinaiticus supplies a separate John 1:1–5 sample only. These are sources for research, not an adopted NTGL critical text.

Tischendorf printed text and apparatus

Archive.org catalogue candidates: volume I (1869) and volume II (1872). Catalogue titles describe an apparatus criticus. Title-page and apparatus images have not yet been checked here. These scans are not the separate 1877 critica minor, and no unverified OCR is installed as an apparatus.

Abbott–Smith historical lexicon

The 1922 lexicon and its TEI markup are declared public domain by the source project. This is historical lexicography, not a modern specialist review. Crosswalks are credited to the Open Scriptures Septuagint Project, CC BY 4.0; their upstream file warns of 87 mismatched mappings.
