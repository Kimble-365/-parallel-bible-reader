# Reader 1.20 — original multi-passage reading paths

Prepared release; browser verification and root promotion remain pending. Includes all 1.19 alignment, gzip fallback, Japanese kana, tracker and source-resource changes. 1.19 passed the complete preview suite in Actions 40642872277 (commit 25671c3969db2384b0af109b87dd5b037540c78b).

Study → Reading plans offers three original paths: Hope & courage (7 days, four daily chapters), Jesus & everyday faith (14 days through Mark and selected letters, NT only), and Prayer, wisdom & service (21 days, three daily chapters with intentional repeated James readings). Their selections/topics are original; no proprietary provider plan, devotional or media is copied. A day can contain several passages; opening each passage retains Text 1 and never marks it completed. Check readings individually or manually mark an available day complete. Continue opens the next unfinished day. Dates are optional; there are no artificial reading-time guarantees or account requirements. Unavailable readings remain disabled rather than borrowing a different slot.

Progress is per plan and follows the reader when translations change. Optional calendar dates use local calendar-day arithmetic without DST drift. The new plan store is separate from notes, bookmarks, chapter tracker and the prior seven-day plan; a button retains access to that earlier plan and its progress. Storage failures are reported; corrupt existing data is not overwritten. This release reuses the immutable 1.18 source/Latin packs and 1.19 kana packs rather than duplicating corpora.

Study → Listening & resources links to a sourced comparison of ten Bible apps and seven text-source categories. See [the online comparison](resources.html) and [research notes](../../docs/research/app-and-text-assessment-120.md). Japanese Kougo-yaku 1954/1955 and Berean Standard Bible are promising additional text candidates with primary rights evidence. They are not installed by the comparison. The catalog remains 62 editions. Exact source acquisition/validation, other guide translations, full Estonian OT, reviewed spoken forms and physical Safari/Edge testing remain pending.
