Printed Swete checks

1 November 2026 · LXXL scope and apparatus

Two printed pages were visually inspected. These are printed-edition checks, not independent collations of ancient manuscripts. No Greek baseline readings have been changed.

  1. The tower-of-Gader journey is already at Greek Genesis 35:16. It appears on printed page 67 with a parenthesized (21) reference and is present in the LXXL baseline. The Reuben episode appears later at Greek 35:21. A statement absent from one numbered slot is not necessarily absent from the chapter.
  2. The 31:51 reference gap belongs to the print. Printed page 60 moves from 50 to 52 and its apparatus reports [51 om ADE]. A missing digital key at this locus must not itself be called an extraction failure. Source-local sigla have not been independently checked against the manuscripts.
  3. The digital 31:55/32:1 boundary is defective. The print starts 32:1 with Jacob's departure. The TEI and downstream digital text instead place the opening phrase at 31:55 and insert the Roman chapter heading into the sentence.
  4. The digital Swete 35:16 omits a printed word. The print includes ἐπέκεινα; the LXXL baseline retains it, while the inspected TEI and downstream Swete text omit it. This is a digital-source error relative to the print, not a newly collated ancient variant.

The installed Swete source remains unchanged, with its provenance preserved. These findings do not authorize global renumbering, replacement of the Greek text from Hebrew, or a claim to have reconstructed the earliest Greek reading.
