# Early printed comparanda for LXXL — research note, 8 November 2026

User asked whether Aldine, Sixtine and Lambert Bos 1709 could help the separate LXXL project. **Yes, as named edition comparanda; not as three independent ancient manuscript witnesses.** No LXXL text, source report, source identity or separate automation output is changed by this note.

The Apostolic Bible publisher's introduction describes use of the Vaticanus–Sixtine family, an Aldine printing, Bos 1709 and Complutensian variants. Its own eclectic selection and AB-Strong interlinear remain a distinct modern work. Agreement of printed editions is not itself manuscript support or sufficient evidence to reconstruct an older reading.

Practical first pass: choose the already defined Genesis loci in LXXL's own current report, locate each in the actual printed page images, transcribe the exact text/accents/punctuation and record edition/date/volume/page/column, persistent image URL and image checksum. For Bos, separate running text from apparatus, identify each quoted edition/manuscript siglum and trace its source where possible. Mark unreadable or unverified items explicitly. Keep these records separate from primary manuscript collations, hand identification and claims of Antiochene/Lucianic affiliation. A specialist should assess the printed editions' dependence before any stemmatic weighting.

Evidence checked:

- ABP introduction, PDF pp. 4–6: publisher copyright 1996/2013 and account of printed comparanda: https://www.apostolicbible.com/intro.pdf . ABP itself has no located public-domain edition or general project redistribution/adaptation grant.
- NYPL, Complutensian Polyglot spread, printed 1514–1517: https://old-digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/899001eb-0c64-394f-e040-e00a18065714 . Explicit US public-domain assessment, image download options. This linked item is a spread, not an imported complete machine-readable corpus.
- Harvard description of the six-volume Complutensian: https://library.hds.harvard.edu/exhibits/incomparable-treasure/complutensian-polyglot . Bibliographic orientation, not a substitute for the original page.
- Bos 1709 institutional catalog: https://www.nationaltrustcollections.org.uk/object/3048785 . Identifies the historical edition; digital transcription/completeness is not established by the catalog record.

Its new pronunciation view is merely a derived reading aid and must not be labelled ABP, Aldine, Sixtine, Bos or a new LXXL witness. No new OCR project or import of these early editions is claimed here.
