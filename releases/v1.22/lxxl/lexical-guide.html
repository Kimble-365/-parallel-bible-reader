Contextual Greek–Hebrew lexical guide

LXXL scope and apparatus · Selected passage links

Seventeen editorial lookup guides for Genesis 1:1–5, based on thirty-four selected alignment records. Headwords and English glosses are editorial aids, not upstream Greek lemmatization or complete dictionary definitions. Every example retains its whole indexed source phrase; neighboring words are not automatically paired with each other.

A zero counterpart is not missing manuscript text. Hebrew prefixes remain within their original source tokens. The phrase-only adjective entry must not be split into invented one-to-one word equivalents. The related apparatus topics are distinct textual units, not additional lexical occurrence counts.

ἀρχή / ראשית

The attested Greek phrase is ἐν ἀρχῇ; Hebrew בראשית includes the prefixed preposition. The entry does not decide the disputed syntactic scope of the opening Hebrew clause, and the Greek preposition alone cannot establish a different Hebrew source text.

ποιέω / ברא

The Greek source form is ἐποίησεν, corresponding locally to Hebrew ברא. This does not make ποιέω a universal equivalent of this Hebrew verb or establish that Greek distinguishes every Hebrew creation verb. The Greek dictionary headword is an editorial assignment, not imported ancient morphological evidence.

θεός / אלהים

Greek article-plus-noun and anarthrous genitive forms occur in the selected contexts. Hebrew אלהים has plural morphology but the narrative agreement here does not require a plural Greek subject. The spirit/wind phrase is quoted as context, not decomposed into an automatically inferred lexical equation. The linked apparatus report concerns omission at Gen 1:4, not a general rule about articles.

οὐρανός / שמים

The Greek accusative singular with article corresponds to Hebrew השמים. Difference in grammatical number can belong to translation idiom and is not by itself evidence for a different consonantal Vorlage. The Hebrew headword omits its attested prefixed article for lookup only; the source tokens retain it.

The selected phrase is πνεῦμα Θεοῦ against רוח אלהים. Both spirit and wind are possible contextual English guides; this entry does not resolve the theological or meteorological interpretation. A translation choice is not automatically a textual variant or proof of a different Hebrew noun.

ἐπιφέρω / רחף

Greek ἐπεφέρετο is an imperfect form; the Hebrew source has a participial expression. The Hebrew lookup root רחף is editorial and differs from the attested inflected מרחפת. The linked source spelling επεφερετω is recorded as an orthographic apparatus report, not a new dictionary lemma or an adopted tense change.

This guide does not reconstruct the translator’s Hebrew source, decide Old Greek readings, or cover the complete Septuagint lexicon. A shared Hebrew lookup key may lead to distinct Greek entries, as with εἰμί and γίνομαι. All registered textual-variant commentaries remain in the separate apparatus.
