# Parallel Bible Reader 1.29

Adds 116 rights-reviewed English and European source editions to the preserved 84-edition Reader 1.27 catalog (200 total). Texts use an independent gzip data group and retain source USFM, rights pages, attribution, exact available book coverage and SHA-256 provenance.

The catalog includes public-domain, Creative Commons, GNU FDL and specifically reviewed noncommercial redistribution texts. No-derivatives editions receive format conversion only; their translated words and punctuation are not revised. Four unclear/incompatible candidates and 13 duplicates are recorded in `sources/europe-english-review.json`. Protected translations remain outside the public bundle.
