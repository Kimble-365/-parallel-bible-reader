LXXL scope and apparatus · Printed-page checks

Twelve provisional editorial content links at Genesis 31/32 and 35. These are separate from the 34 Genesis 1 phrase alignments. They do not form a complete versification map or establish word-by-word equivalence. Source-local references and original text order are retained.

Greek 35:16 contains both Gader-journey material corresponding in content to Hebrew 35:21 and childbirth material corresponding to part of Hebrew 35:16. A single verse-number offset would conceal this arrangement.

Excerpts join indexed token displays; punctuation and XML segmentation are not reproduced diplomatically. Download compressed token IDs, source hashes and commentary.
