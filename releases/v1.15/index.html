About pronunciation forms

An experimental US English phoneme estimate is available for WEB through each Text form selector. It is dictionary-based, not a recording transcript. Unknown words remain in brackets; stress, syllables and contextual pronunciation are not resolved. Other languages retain written transliteration only until a suitable pronunciation resource is verified.

Each text is optional. Choose None to hide that text; the other numbered choices stay in place.

        Search the text

        Search help

        Search covers the texts and forms selected in the reader. All words finds words together in one verse; use Contains text for word endings or Exact phrase for a quotation. One letter or number is enough. Finnish and Hungarian letters and Indic vowel signs are kept distinct. Unless exact marks is selected, Hebrew/Arabic vowel marks and Greek/transliteration accents are simplified for matching; displayed Bible text is unchanged.

        First searches may download books. Repeated searches reuse a local word index. An unavailable book does not stop the remaining search, and incomplete coverage is reported. Saved books only stays within downloaded text. Show more reveals results beyond the first 100.

          My notes

          Bible quotations are copied from Text 1 with their source reference and translation name. Your own writing stays separate. Notes are saved only in this browser; export a backup to keep them.

          Add Bible text from Text 1

          Use a three-letter book code or English book name, for example JHN 3:16-18 or John 3:16. References follow the selected edition's numbering. Changing Text 1 does not change previously saved quotations.

          Open in Google Docs or Google Slides

          Download Word (.docx) or slides (.pptx) above, then open Google Docs or Google Slides. Choose File → Open → Upload and select your downloaded file. To create Google's own editable format, choose File → Save as Google Docs or Save as Google Slides. You can also upload the file to Google Drive and open it with the corresponding app.

          A Google account and internet are needed for Google services. The reader creates files on your device and never signs in or uploads your notes automatically. JSON remains the backup format for restoring notes into this reader.

          Restore a notes backup

          Imports are added to your notebook without replacing existing notes. Nothing is uploaded. The notebook holds up to 100 notes within a 2 MB storage limit.
