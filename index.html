    Language availability, sources and licensing

    Only installed texts can be selected. External sources open on their publisher’s website; their transliterations are not included in this reader.

    Language / editionCoverage and statusSource

    Transliterations are mechanical conversions of written scripts, not translations or pronunciation guides. Hindi uses the project’s IAST-style convention; the Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Odia and Gujarati editions use Aksharamukha ISO Latin. The installed Urdu edition uses Devanagari script. Specialist linguistic review is pending. Arabic-script Urdu uses ICU orthographic Latin conversion; unwritten short vowels are not reconstructed. Keep the original text visible when comparing.

    Parallel display uses the source book, chapter and verse numbers. Verse divisions may differ between editions. A combined verse retains its original range and is not duplicated across verse numbers.
