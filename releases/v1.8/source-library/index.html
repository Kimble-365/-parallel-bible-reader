Installed texts include written Latin transliterations where applicable. These are not pronunciation guides. Independent source panels preserve chapter and verse keys, including Esther’s prologue, Odes iva/ivb and labels such as 59a and 1a1. Equal labels do not establish equivalent passages.

Modern Hebrew New Testament — external reading

Open Haktuvim. The current revision date and permission to redistribute or transliterate its modern Hebrew NT have not been verified. No modern text has been copied into this reader. The installed historical Delitzsch text is a separate edition.

Brenton’s English Septuagint — external reading

Read the historical edition at CCEL. Brenton’s nineteenth-century translation is public domain. The available flattened JSON mixes footnotes into some verses; it was not installed as clean verse text. The installed Thomson selection is an alternative English translation and currently covers only 11 source books.

Installed Greek editions

Swete: 52 source books; Ecclesiastes is absent from this digital corpus. Esdras B retains combined Ezra–Nehemiah source chapters. Old Greek Daniel, Susanna and Bel are separate from the three-book Theodotion option. Source CC BY-SA 4.0.

Vamvas 1850: 66 books in historical formal Greek (Katharevousa). Its Old Testament comes from Hebrew; it is not presented as a Septuagint translation.

Installed translations of the Septuagint and its tradition

French Giguet and other translators (1872): 54 nonempty source books. Empty NT placeholders and Enoch are excluded from the LXX reading selection. Church Slavonic Elizabeth Bible: 76 nonempty source books, with an Old Testament in the Septuagint tradition. Both digital modules declare public domain.

English Thomson (1808): 11 completed source files. In-progress Exodus and Proverbs are excluded. Its transcription uses KJV-style numbering; no verified verse mapping is claimed.

Historical Hebrew sources and quality

Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures: 39 books, CC BY 4.0. Delitzsch historical NT: 27 books, from 260 attributed Wikisource chapter revisions, CC BY-SA 4.0. This is not the revised 2003 edition or a claim to the latest modern Hebrew NT.

Some Wikisource chapters begin with an unnumbered passage; the reader labels it 0, “unnumbered source opening”, rather than inventing verse 1. Matthew 15:32 is absent from the source revision. Other source numbering anomalies remain unchanged. Consult the raw revision archive for textual work.

Further candidates

Historical Russian Yungerov translations merit further source and rights verification. Modern NETS, Septuaginta Deutsch, La Bible d’Alexandrie and modern Romanian projects require edition-specific redistribution permission; public web access alone does not supply it. No unverified text or audio match has been installed.
